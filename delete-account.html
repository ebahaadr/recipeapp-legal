Delete Your Account

Recipeo, by Summit Core Technologies LLC

You can permanently delete your Recipeo account and the data associated with it at any time. There is no waiting period and no need to contact us first — you can do it yourself from inside the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Recipeo app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. Once it is done, the account cannot be recovered, and signing in again with the same Google or Apple ID creates a new, empty account.

If you cannot open the app

If you have lost access to your device or can no longer sign in, email info@summitcoretechnologies.com from the email address on the account, with the subject Delete my Recipeo account. We will verify the request and delete the account, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes all of the following:

  • Your account record, name, email address, username, and profile — including your bio and any links you added
  • Your profile photo and any photos you uploaded of food you cooked
  • Recipes you created or published, and the photos attached to them
  • Recipes generated for you by the AI
  • Recipes you saved
  • Your preferences and cooking profile
  • Your pantry, shopping list, meal plan, and food diary entries
  • Your follows, followers, and any accounts you blocked
  • Reports you submitted, and reports submitted about you
  • Your subscription history and usage records held by us
  • Your device's push notification token

What is kept, and for how long

  • Your subscription itself is not cancelled by deleting your account. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or Apple, not by us, and they keep their own records. To stop being charged, cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store or in your Apple ID settings before deleting your account.
  • Records held by our service providers. Google Play, Apple, and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their own policies and their own retention periods, which we do not control.
  • Moderation notifications. When someone reports content, we receive an email alert. Those alert emails are not part of the app's database and may remain in our mailbox after the account is deleted, for as long as we need them to keep the app safe and to meet our legal obligations.
  • Backups. Deleted data can persist in routine database backups for a short period before those backups roll over. It is not restored to the app.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove things. From inside the app you can delete individual recipes and the photos on them, unpublish a recipe you shared, remove saved recipes, clear your shopping list, and delete pantry items, meal plan entries, and food diary entries. You can also unfollow accounts and turn off notifications, which removes your device's push token.
